Song Sparrow Song

Melospiza melodia is an incredible model system for studying the development and adaptive function of song. For my honor's thesis project in Steve Nowicki's Lab at Duke University, I used an incredible longitudinal dataset of recordings from hand-reared song sparrow males to assess whether individuals exhibit evidence of delayed maturation in song. I found strong evidence for differences between the first and second years of life, but not thereafter. I later tested whether males notice and respond to these differences with a playback experiment at the Pymatuning Lab of Ecology in northwestern Pennsylvania.

Kochvar, K. H., Peters, S., Zipple, M. N., & Nowicki, S. (2022). Maturational changes in song sparrow song. Journal of Avian Biology. https://doi.org/10.1111/jav.02872
