Atlantic Puffin Bill Coloration

Fratercula arctica are known for their colorful red-orange bills, which both males and females sport during the breeding season and shed during the winter, nonbreeding season. This incredible annual investment suggests that bill color could play an important role in communication between individuals within the colony. During my M.Sc. at the Memorial University of Newfoundland, I studied whether puffin bill color could function as an honest signal of parental ability and investment between mated pairs in Pierre-Paul Bitton's Visual Ecology Lab. To answer this question, I studied temporal variability of bill color, offspring growth models, links between parental bill color and offspring growth, and assortative mating based on bill color and morphology.
