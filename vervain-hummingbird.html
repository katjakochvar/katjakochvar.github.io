Vervain Hummingbird Song

Mellisuga minima is the second smallest hummingbird species in the world, native to the Caribbean islands of Hispaniola and Jamaica. Although visually drab - males lack the brilliant iridescent gorget feathers typical to most hummingbirds in the Bee clade and appear virtually identical to females - they make up for it with an incredibly complex song. Since 2024, I have been studying Vervain hummingbird song in Punta Cana, DR to better understand how this complexity is formed and maintained at a small spatial scale. This work is also providing insight into the vocal learning capabilities of this species, including beyond the first year of life (i.e., open-ended learning).
